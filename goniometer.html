Goniometer Normal Viewer

Set the base, arm and spin angles to see where the sample's surface normal n points in the lab frame (Z up). Angles are positive by the right-hand rule: looking down the axis vector toward the sample, positive turns counter-clockwise. n is a unit vector, so its tip sits on the unit sphere.
